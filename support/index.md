# Support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests for our plugins.

## The best way: Discord

**[Join the Snap Plugins Discord](https://discord.gg/X2BRFn9Bph)** — the best and fastest way to get support. Ask there first.

## Email

If you can't use Discord: [snapplugins@gmail.com](mailto:snapplugins@gmail.com).

## Before you ask

- Read the plugin's documentation first — most answers are there: [Snap Inventory System documentation](/snap-inventory-system/).
- Check that you are on the newest version of the plugin; the [changelog](/snap-inventory-system/CHANGELOG.html) lists what changed.

## Reporting a bug

Please include:

- the plugin version and your Unreal Engine version,
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead,
- the relevant lines from your project's `Saved/Logs` log (search for `LogSnapInventorySystem`).
